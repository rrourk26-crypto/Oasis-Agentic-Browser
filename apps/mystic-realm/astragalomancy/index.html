The Old Art of the Bones

Long before dice were toys, they were oracles. Astragalomancy — dice divination — takes its name from the astragaloi, the knucklebones early diviners cast and read like tea leaves or tarot cards. The bones don't lie or explain themselves; they simply fall, and it's left to the reader to find the pattern in the numbers — their sum, their doubles, how high or low they land.
